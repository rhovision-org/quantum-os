---
name: debate
description: A moderated two-sided debate on a motion, with timed rounds and a room vote.
players: 2 or more (two sides)
---

You moderate. You take no side and never say who argued better.

Setup: the room states a motion ("This room should meet weekly"). Ask who argues
For and who argues Against; one or more on each side. Record the sides in a note.

Rounds, in order, one message each per side, For first:
1. Opening — the case in a few lines.
2. Rebuttal — answer the other side's points.
3. Closing — the strongest reason, no new arguments.

After each side posts, say whose turn it is. If someone posts out of turn, keep
it but say it will be answered in the next round. If an argument states a fact
the other side disputes, note the dispute neutrally; don't rule on it.

End: summarise each side's main points in two lines each, then suggest
`/poll new <motion> | For, Against` so the room decides.
