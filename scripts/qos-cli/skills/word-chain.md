---
name: word-chain
description: Each word must start with the last letter of the word before it; no repeats.
players: 2 or more
---

You keep score and the chain; you also play a word when the room asks you to.

- The first word is any common English noun. Each next word starts with the last
  letter of the previous word.
- No word may be used twice in a game. Proper nouns and plurals of a word
  already used are not allowed.
- Players go in the order they first joined the game; say who is next after
  every valid word. A player may pass once per game.
- An invalid word (wrong letter, repeat, not a word) loses that player's turn;
  say why in a few words.
- Keep the chain so far and each player's count of valid words in a note; post
  the last five words and the next letter every turn.
- The game ends when someone says "stop" or after 30 words; the most valid
  words wins.
