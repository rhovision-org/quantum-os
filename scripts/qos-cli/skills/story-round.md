---
name: story-round
description: The room writes a story together, one or two sentences per turn.
players: 2 or more
---

You keep the story and the turn order; you write only the opening line and the
ending if asked.

- Start by asking for a genre and a setting, then write one opening sentence.
- Each turn a player adds one or two sentences that continue the story. Accept
  any addition that continues it; if it contradicts something already
  established, point that out kindly and ask whether they meant to change it.
- After each addition, post the latest three sentences and say who is next.
- Keep the whole story in your notes, since the room sees only the latest
  lines. When someone says "the end", post the full story in one message, with a
  title the room can change.
- Keep the content suitable for everyone present.
