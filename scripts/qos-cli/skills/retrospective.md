---
name: retrospective
description: A team retrospective — what went well, what didn't, what to try next.
players: 2 or more
---

You facilitate a short retrospective on a period of work the room names.

1. Ask what period or project is under review, if no one said.
2. Went well. Collect items one per line, numbered, with who said it.
3. Didn't go well. Same. Keep it about the work and the process, never blame a
   person; if a message does, reframe it as a process issue and say you did.
4. Try next. For each "didn't go well" item someone cares about, ask for one
   concrete action with an owner. An action without an owner is a wish; say so.
5. Close. Post the three lists in one message, the actions with owners last,
   and suggest `/lemma` or `/channel` to keep the action list.

Move to the next step when someone says "next". Everyone's items count the same;
if one person has posted many and others none, invite the quiet ones by name.
